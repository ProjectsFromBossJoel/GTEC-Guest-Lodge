How verification works

  1. Get the Guest ID and roomThe guest has both in their booking e-mail and SMS. Coming from the Check-In page, they are filled in for you.
  2. Scan or typeIf the guest shows a QR code, hold it to the camera and the fields fill in by themselves.
  3. Press Verify GuestThis confirms the ID and room match and marks the guest as verified.
  4. Press Check In GuestThe guest becomes Checked In and the room turns Occupied.
Good to know
Guest IDs look like GH-12345. GH- is already in the box, so type only the numbers. The room number must match the booking exactly.

“Already Verified” means this guest was verified earlier. You can still check them in.

The camera needs your permission, and works only on a secure page (https or localhost).
